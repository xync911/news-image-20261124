A front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image (1024×1024 PNG).

**Headlines:** ICJ finds Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide; NATO vows defence in space as Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite; Ukraine confirms sea-drone strikes on Russian ships off Libya; Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang and markets surge; Russia warns Estonia of war; Israeli strikes kill six in Gaza.

**Hero:** the Peace Palace in The Hague, seat of the International Court of Justice.

`main.py` copies `news.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The program is deterministic: two runs write identical bytes.
